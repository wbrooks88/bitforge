Pixel editor

Black

The black outline is added around the edge automatically. New pixels are added to both sides so the front and back keep the same shape. Pixels that don't touch the figure are dropped when printing.

Describe your hero

Try words like race (elf, orc, tiefling), class (ranger, wizard, bard, knight, berserker), monsters (skeleton, zombie, cultist, lich), gear (axe, bow, torch, hood, crown) and colors ("blue robe", "blonde hair", "silver armor").

Character

Upscaled art is detected and shrunk back to its real pixels. Near-black pixels become the black plate; up to 120×120 pixels.

Paint over any figure, front and back.

Colors

Very dark colors are lifted automatically so they don't disappear into the black outline, and blades print a shade brighter than armor.

Print settings

Minis are flat pixel standees: a black outline plate with the colored pixels raised a few layers on the front. Print the figure lying on its back (colors only change in the top layers), then click its tab into the slot in the base. Files are in millimetres, Z up; 3MF and OBJ keep colors for multi-material printers.
