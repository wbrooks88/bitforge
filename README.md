# Bitforge Mini Maker

Turn a short description, a hand-drawn pose, or your own pixel art into a flat, pixel-art standee mini for tabletop RPGs, ready for 3D printing.

**Use it:** https://wbrooks88.github.io/bitforge/

- 13 shaded pixel-art figures (9 heroes: fighter, rogue, wizard, cleric, ranger, barbarian, bard, berserker, knight; 4 monsters: skeleton, zombie, cultist, lich), front and back
- Mix & match: recolor any figure (metal, cloth, skin and more) and swap heads from a hand-checked list of combos that fit; image upload (large images are scaled down automatically) and a pixel editor
- Colored front and back, black outline plate, click-in base with three fit tolerances and a fit test kit
- Tuned 5-filament palettes (black included) plus a max-colors option
- Exports STL, 3MF (one part per filament for PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer and Bambu Studio) and OBJ, at 28, 32 or 54 mm scale

Everything runs in the browser from the single `index.html` file; there is no backend.
